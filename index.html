What we stand for

Farmers should not have to wait for good tools to reach them.

Climate pressure is real and the people closest to the land feel it first. We are building practical help for them, starting in Botswana.

  1. 01

    Knowledge within reach

    Farmers make better decisions when they have the right information. It should not depend on where you live, what you earn or how comfortable you are with technology.

  2. 02

    Practical over flashy

    New ideas are only worth it if they solve a real problem in a real field. We judge our work by whether it makes someone's livelihood better.

  3. 03

    Built with communities

    We work alongside farmers, organisations and partners, and we protect the land so the next generation can farm it too.

Where we are

Early days, and honest about it.

GrowBase is at the planning stage. We would rather build slowly with the people we serve than make big promises. If you farm, advise farmers or fund agricultural work in Botswana, we would like to hear from you.
