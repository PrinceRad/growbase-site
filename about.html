About GrowBase

Practical help for the people who feed us.

GrowBase is an agro-innovation initiative bringing together technology, climate-smart agriculture and community-driven solutions, starting in Botswana.

Our mission

To empower smallholder farmers with innovative, accessible and sustainable solutions that strengthen food security, improve livelihoods and build resilience to climate change.

Our vision

A climate-resilient future where every farmer has the knowledge, technology and resources needed to thrive.

Our purpose

We connect agriculture, technology and climate innovation to create practical solutions for the farmers who need them most.

Our values

What guides the work.

01

Innovation

We use technology and new ideas to solve real agricultural challenges.

02

Sustainability

We promote farming practices that protect natural resources and support future generations.

03

Empowerment

Farmers should have the knowledge, tools and opportunities to make informed decisions.

04

Accessibility

Innovation should not be limited by location, income or digital experience.

05

We build solutions together with farmers, organisations, communities and partners.

06

Impact

We focus on practical solutions that create real improvements in people's lives.

Motho ke motho ka batho. A person is a person through other people. It is how we want to work.
